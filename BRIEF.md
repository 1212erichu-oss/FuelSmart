# Project brief: FuelSmart

## The competition
- I’m entering the Presidential SI Challenge (Standard Track), a national K–12 competition. My age group: Middle school (grades 6–8)
- Judges score only a written entry (a PDF of up to 10 pages) and a video of up to 4 minutes. They reward a clear understanding of the problem and a sensible use of AI.
- My deadlines: Registration Deadline at December 18 and the registration that the teacher has to do

## The problem
- People in my school struggle with losing weight because the school gives unhealthy food.
- Impact area: Environment
- Why it matters (impact): It will track other people's diet and help them lose weight.
- Evidence it’s real: 1 in 5 people have obesity https://www.cdc.gov/school-health-conditions/chronic/obesity.html
- What people told me: Person A said that the lunch sucks and never gets it.

## My solution
- My AI’s job: My AI helps by giving the people a balanced diet, which works better than throwing away their lunch because it gives them energy to last the day.
- Tools that already exist, and how mine is different: Our school has different lunch options and the option to bring food but ours is different because we tell them what to eat and how it helps them.
- Safety plan: AI could give them the wrong food to eat or give them bad advice.

## My MVP features (build these first, in this order)
1. The user can enter today's lunch options and get a balanced AI meal pick.
2. The user can see why each food they pick gives them energy.
3. The user can build a plate and see if it has all food groups.
4. The AI gives correct, safe answers to real questions.

## Maybe later (not yet)
- The user can log what they ate today and get a simple balance check.
- The user can ask the AI for healthy snack ideas to bring from home.
- The user can list foods they are allergic to so the AI avoids them.
- The user can see a reminder to check diet changes with a doctor.
- The user can plan a week of lunches with the AI.
- The user can swap an unhealthy food for a healthier choice the AI suggests.
- The user can track how many glasses of water they drink.
- The user can report AI food advice that seems wrong.

## My AI’s instructions
- When it answers: The user types an item and presses Check.
- Its job: To give the user a balanced food schedule with their preferences and budget.
- Who it talks to and how to sound: Talking to the user and sound like a friend talking to them.
- Facts it must use: School lunches are unhealthy, so we made this website to change it. Type in your preferences and budget and we'll do the rest.
- Rules it must follow: Give truthful, safe information. Never make up medical or nutrition facts.
- How to answer: It should be a chart, and it should come first and try to make it short.
